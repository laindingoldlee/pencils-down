Put the announcer recordings for Black, White & Eleven in this folder, named:

  single-kill.mp3
  double-kill.mp3
  triple-kill.mp3
  quadra-kill.mp3
  pentakill.mp3   (5 kills in one turn)
  godlike.mp3     (6 or more kills in one turn)
  executed.mp3    (knocked out by a called bluff)
  first-blood.mp3 (first knock-out of the game)
  ace.mp3         (the knock-out that leaves one player standing)
  slain.mp3       (heard only by the player who was knocked out)

.m4a, .wav and .ogg work too. Any that are missing just show the words on screen
with a short built-in sound. The server picks them up the next time it starts.
