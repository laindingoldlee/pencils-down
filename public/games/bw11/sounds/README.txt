Put the announcer recordings for Black, White & Eleven in this folder, named:

  single-kill.mp3
  double-kill.mp3
  triple-kill.mp3
  quadra-kill.mp3
  godlike.mp3     (5 or more kills in one turn)
  executed.mp3    (knocked out by a called bluff)

.m4a, .wav and .ogg work too. Any that are missing just show the words on screen
with a short built-in sound. The server picks them up the next time it starts.
